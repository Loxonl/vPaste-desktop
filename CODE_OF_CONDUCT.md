# Code of Conduct

vPaste welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not expose another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation without harassment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. To report a conduct issue, ask a maintainer for a private contact without posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.
